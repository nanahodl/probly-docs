# Get Started

Signing up on Probly takes less than a minute. Click on "Get Started" to choose your preferred sign-in method.

If you’re new to crypto, don’t worry—if you sign up with your email or a social account, a wallet address is automatically created for you. **No wallet setup or browser extension is required**.

## Creating Your Account

1. Go to [probly.com](https://probly.com)
2. Click **Get Started** in the top right
3. Pick a sign-in method, then sign or verify to confirm
4. Accept the [Terms of Use](/broken/pages/4u1zW8xhLNtrPaXxfwaR) and [Privacy Policy](/broken/pages/UO4QgktgNh5GvhrPZeTh)
5. Deposit USDC into your wallet to begin trading

## Sign up/Login Methods

Probly supports both familiar sign-in methods and existing crypto wallets, powered by [Privy](https://privy.io/)

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt="" width="370"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### **Option A — Email**

1. Enter your email address and click **Continue.**
2. We’ll send a 6-digit verification code to your inbox. Enter the code to continue.
3. Privy automatically creates a wallet address tied to your email. You'll deposit `USDC` to this address.
4. Click **Enable Trading** and approve the confirmation that appears. This doesn’t cost anything—it simply activates trading on your account.
5. Deposit `USDC` into your wallet to start trading.

#### **Option B - Social Login (Google, X, etc.)**

1. **Click** on the social login platform of your choice
2. Authorise the login on the social login platform
3. Privy automatically creates a wallet address tied to your email. You'll deposit `USDC` to this address.
4. Click **Enable Trading** and approve the confirmation that appears. This doesn’t cost anything—it simply activates trading on your account.
5. Deposit `USDC` into your wallet to start trading.

#### **Option C  — Crypto Wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, etc.)**

Connect a self-custody wallet. You hold your own keys and sign every transaction.&#x20;

1. Click **Connect** and choose your wallet from the available list. Approve the connection request in the popup.
2. Click **Enable Trading** and approve the confirmation and sign the gas-less transaction.
3. Send `USDC` to your wallet to start trading.

{% hint style="info" %}
We recommend using a standard **Externally Owned Account (EOA) wallet** — such as MetaMask.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** **Account Abstraction (AA) wallets are not currently supported.** Depositing from an AA wallet may prevent subsequent trading and/or withdrawals, and funds could become inaccessible (funds may become permanently locked in the platform contract).
{% endhint %}

## Comparison

|              | Crypto Wallet           | Email / Social Login |
| ------------ | ----------------------- | -------------------- |
| Onboarding   | Connect existing wallet | Verify email         |
| Keys held by | You                     | Privy (encrypted)    |
| Recovery     | Seed phrase             | Email                |
| Gas          | You pay                 | Handled for you      |

## Account Security

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Never share** your seed phrase, private key, or email verification code. Probly will never ask for them via DM, email, or Discord.
{% endhint %}

* Keep your email account secure — we send one-time codes there to log you in
* For self-custody wallets, back up your seed phrase offline
* Always verify you're on **probly.com** before connecting your wallet
* Report suspicious activity to **support@probly.com**
